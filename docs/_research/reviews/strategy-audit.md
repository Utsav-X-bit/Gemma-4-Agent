# Strategy audit — the playbook as an executable plan

**Audit date:** 2026-10-24. **Auditor scope:** plan coherence, ordering, dependencies, contradictions, realism, decision rules, compute usage, first-timer gaps. Claim-level source verification is a separate review (`source-audit`); this file only checks whether the *plan* holds together and can be executed by one person with 4×A100 40 GB plus Kaggle free GPUs by **2026-12-02 23:59 UTC**.

**Inputs read:** `README.md`, `docs/00`–`07`, `docs/SOURCES.md`, and the local `harness_readme_raw.md` / `getting_started_notebook.txt` where a doc claim needed a tie-break.

**Severity scale**

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P0** | Can lose a submission, or makes the plan's own critical path impossible. Fix before anything else. |
| **P1** | Burns a week or a submission slot; decision rule is statistically or operationally wrong. |
| **P2** | Real inefficiency or missing dependency; costs days, not the competition. |
| **P3** | Polish/consistency; fix in a final pass. |

---

## 1. Verdicts on the seven questions

1. **Order — mostly right, wrong at both ends.** Prompt-only → calibrated eval → trajectories → LoRA → adapter validation → selection is the correct dependency chain. But the plan puts local infrastructure *before* the first submission (README §4 Path A, `04` §3–§4) when the public 0.24 zip needs no infrastructure and can be scored on day 1. And it ends without a freeze date or a defined selection procedure. Two dependencies are missing entirely: per-task setup time in the 12 h projection (F03) and a hardware derating from A100 to 4×L4 (F22).
2. **Missing steps/blockers.** No Docker sandbox build (F07), no wheelhouse pinning for adapter validation (F21), no HF gated-model access or rules acceptance (F25), no failure-taxonomy pass before training (F28), no trajectory-generation cost estimate (F27), no prompt freeze across train/eval (F17).
3. **Contradictions.** Six that matter: 8-min vs 5–6-min cap (F02); 4 096-token training vs 7k–17k-token trajectories (F05); trajectory counts that cannot exist (F04); alpha sweep that includes a documented-degenerate scale (F10); graph tools declared-but-discouraged while the doc says remove them (F18); `timeout_seconds` claimed to affect Phase-2 pytest when it does not (F09). Plus the paper "parallel, non-conflicting" claim (F14).
4. **Six-week plan — not realistic as written.** It is 42 days against 39 available, and the paper (Nov 12) and entry (Nov 25) deadlines land inside weeks 3 and 5 with no calendar mapping (F01). Week 4 bundles first adapter + rank/alpha sweeps + paired eval + submission, which is roughly double the capacity once the 14–33 h queue is counted. Cut list in §3.
5. **Stop criteria — directional, not actionable.** `03` §12 has one gate that always fires (F04), one unquantified ("threatens the 12 h limit"), and no dates or infra gate (F15). `06` §3.4's "≥2-task win" is inside noise at typical discordant counts (F13).
6. **Overpromise vs underuse — both.** Prose correctly says post-training is optional, but the calendar makes it the critical path and gives the two highest-EV levers (budget fit, a second diverse submission) one week each. Meanwhile the 4×A100 is treated as four independent single-GPU boxes: no serving allocation (F11), no seed replication for the noise floor, no role for the extra GPUs during evaluation, no distillation plan despite rules allowing it. Kaggle GPUs are named as a resource and never assigned a job (F23).
7. **First-timer gaps.** Rules acceptance/joining and the entry deadline have no checklist step (F25); final-selection mechanics are only half described (F16); licensing inventory is missing (F24); `submission.parquet` is unexplained (F19); the sample submission ships dummy adapters that get registered by the scorer (F20).

## 3. What to cut when time is short

Cut in this order; each item is independent of the baseline.

1. **Paper track** unless re-scoped by Nov 1 to weeks-1–2 evidence (F14). Highest time cost, most subjective payoff.
2. **E4B training.** Keep at most one prompt-only E4B probe (F16). Training E4B buys a bonus lane that a 0.06-bar field already occupies.
3. **FSDP2 / multi-GPU training** (F11). Single-GPU QLoRA on one card, three cards serving/parallel evals.
4. **RL, SRFT, SWE-Prime, distillation** (`03` §10). None has competition evidence; all are post-SFT research bets.
5. **Graph-tool experiments** beyond one 15-task E6 run (F18).
6. **32 k sequences and rank ≥32.** Both are stretch goals with documented instability.
7. **Multi-seed evaluation** down to 2 seeds on Holdout A only (keep F13's discordant-pair floor).
8. **The 127/129 calibration** (F08). 10–15 tasks is enough to trust a comparison.
9. **Alpha sweep** down to two points, 0.5 and 1.0 (F10).
10. **Trajectory generation at 4 temperatures** down to 2 (F27).

Never cut: the day-1 probe submission (F06), the setup-time measurement (F03), the 5-min primary bundle (F02), the adapter A/B at temperature 0 (`03` §9.3), the two-slot diversity rule (`04` §10.4).

## 4. Minimum viable path if only 10 days remain

Assume the window is **Nov 22 → Dec 2** (deadline 23:59 UTC). The paper deadline has passed; the entry deadline (Nov 25) is **day 4** — join and accept rules on day 1 or you are not ranked. Assume no adapter exists and the local evaluator is not trusted.

**Rules for the 10 days:** one variable per submission; never let a day pass with an empty submission queue; every bundle is reproducible from a manifest; no training unless the baseline is already scored twice.

|---|---|---|---|

**10-day cuts:** no LoRA, no trajectory dataset, no adapter validation, no FSDP, no graph-tool study, no paper, no E4B training, no 40-task holdout, no multi-seed.

**If day 1's probe scores ≥0.20:** spend days 4–8 only on cap/thinking/prompt variants and slot diversity; skip the E4B probe entirely.
**If day 1's probe fails or errors:** stop all other work until the submission mechanism and the bundle validate; a second failed probe is worse than a mediocre score.
**If the queue exceeds 24 h:** assume one scored run per 2 days and pre-commit both finalists by day 7, not day 9.

## 5. Arithmetic appendix (all checks reproducible)

- Deadline window: 2026-10-24 → 2026-12-02 = **39 days = 5.57 weeks**; the README's six weeks ends **2026-12-05**.
- Paper deadline is **19 days** out; entry deadline **32 days** out.
