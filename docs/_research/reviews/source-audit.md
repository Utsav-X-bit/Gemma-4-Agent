## Top 10 fixes

1. **Fix the 240 s / 300 s conflict (#2).** `evaluation.timeout_seconds` is Phase-1 only; Phase-2 pytest is fixed at 300 s (S5:506). Delete the "shorter timeout fails a correct patch" rationale in 02 §2.1/§2.3/§10 and flag S38's claim as contradicted by the official harness. This is the one finding that currently teaches a false mechanism.
2. **Collapse the budget guidance to one primary (#3).** 5 min / 24 calls / thinking 2048 everywhere; 8/28/4096 becomes "reference config, A-B only". Update README §9 and 02 §2/§10, which still ship the old default.
3. **Qualify the 0.24 result everywhere (#1).** S36 is author-reported, its sidebar shows 0.18, and the notebook does not run inference. 02 §2.3's rationale belongs to S38. The strategy is unchanged, but the premise must stop reading as verified.
4. **Correct the per-task score step (#4).** Final standings are over the private ~60, so one task ≈0.017, not 0.0083. This doubles the apparent noise floor of every decision rule in 06.
5. **Re-source or delete the S28 statistics (#5).** The 7-of-8 timeouts and `exit_loop` claims are not in S28; `exit_loop` belongs to S5:341. Structural advice resting on a fabricated statistic is worse than no statistic.
6. **Derive the LoRA serving parameters (#6).** `max_loras`/`max_lora_rank` come from the adapter map (S5:153). Hard-coding 16 makes the local A/B a different stack from the scorer for rank>16 adapters.
7. **Reconcile the S25 numbers (#7).** "Five temperatures", "12,602 tokens", "2.5k–14.7k chars" are the load-bearing evidence for the thinking-mismatch lesson and appear nowhere in the ledger; add quotes or remove them.
8. **Use `gemma-4-thinking` in the 31B recipes (#8).** As written, §7.1/§7.2 generate no-thought data while §4.2 requires thinking-matched data — the exact failure S25 documents.
9. **Unify the alpha sweep (#9).** 0.5/1.0 as candidates, 2.0 as a negative control only. README §4 and §7 currently disagree.
10. **Canonicalise the training-base IDs and verify them (#10).** `google/gemma-4-31B` vs `google/gemma-4-31B-it` vs `unsloth/gemma-4-31B-it`; none of the unquantized-QAT IDs is source-verified. One ID per size, with a ledger entry carrying URL + revision.
